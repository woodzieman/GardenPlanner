🌿 3D Garden Planner

Capture, map, and plan your outdoor growing spaces — using just your iPhone.

Available on the App Store

How It Works

1

Scan

Walk around your garden with your iPhone. LiDAR-enabled models create a 3D point cloud in seconds.

2

Map

The app automatically extracts a 2D top-down garden boundary and elevation heightmap.

3

Plan

Place plants, set companion pairs, track harvests, and get frost-date alerts for your zone.

Key Features

📷

LiDAR Garden Scanning

On iPhone Pro models, capture your garden space with depth data and LiDAR for accurate garden maps.

🌱

121 Plant Database

Comprehensive database of 50 vegetables, 71 flowers, and herbs with planting guides.

📦

Barcode Scanning

Scan seed packet barcodes (GP#####00 format) to quickly add plants to your garden.

🌡️

Frost Alerts

Custom frost-date calculator with 7-day weather forecasts and planting window recommendations.

☁️

CloudKit Sync

Optional, encrypted sync across your Apple devices. Your data stays in your iCloud.

📓

Journals & Harvests

Per-plant journal entries, task lists, yield logs, and seasonal analytics.

Start Planning Your Garden Today

Free to use. No account required. All data stays on your device.

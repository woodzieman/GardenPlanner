Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

3D Garden Planner ("we", "our", "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we handle data on your device.

1. Data Collected

The app collects and stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Garden data: Garden layouts, plant placements, planting zones, and garden plans
  • Plant records: Journal entries, task lists, harvest records, and seed records
  • Profile information: Your USDA growing zone, location, and personal preferences
  • Scan data: LiDAR scans and AR capture data processed entirely on-device
  • Weather data: Your location used for local weather forecasts (from Open-Meteo)

No data is collected by our servers. All data remains on your device by default.

2. Data Shared

CloudKit Sync (Optional)

If you enable CloudKit sync, your garden data is encrypted and synced to your personal Apple iCloud container. Apple may process this data to provide sync services. You can disable sync at any time in Settings.

Weather API

The app sends your latitude and longitude to Open-Meteo (a free, no-key-required weather API) to retrieve local forecasts. Your location is not stored by us — it is only used to fetch weather data each time you view the Weather tab. Open-Meteo's privacy policy applies: https://open-meteo.com/

CloudKit Account (Optional)

If you enable CloudKit sync, your Apple ID and team ID are used to authenticate and store data in your iCloud container. This is required by Apple's CloudKit API.

3. Data Stored Locally

All garden plans, journal entries, harvest records, tasks, weather data, and LiDAR scan data are stored locally in your device's SwiftData store. This data can be deleted at any time via the app's data management settings.

4. LiDAR & Camera Usage

Camera and LiDAR data is captured and processed entirely on your device. No camera feeds, photos, or point clouds are transmitted to any server. The app uses camera access for:

  • Barcode scanning (seed packets)
  • LiDAR-based outdoor mapping (on supported devices)
  • Manual map editing (on non-LiDAR devices)

5. Children's Privacy

This app is not specifically designed for children, but there are no features that collect personal information from children. All data is stored locally and does not require account creation.

6. Data Deletion

You can delete all stored data at any time from within the app's Settings view. Deleting your garden data removes it from both the device and your iCloud (if sync is enabled).

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
